Data Request & Deletion Policy

Meta Platform Compliant Instructions • Effective Date: August 12, 2026

At Sahajta AI Solution Pvt Ltd (“Sahajta AI”), we respect your right to privacy and give you full control over your personal data. In compliance with Meta Platform Terms, CCPA, and global privacy standards, you can request access to or permanent deletion of your personal data stored by our systems.

How to Request Data Deletion

If you wish to have your lead information, contact details, or scheduling records deleted, please follow these simple steps:

  1. Email Data Request: Send an email to our Data Compliance Officer at shubhang@sahajta.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include Verification Details: Include your full name, email address, and company name used when interacting with our site or intake modal.
  3. Processing & Confirmation: Our team will confirm receipt within 48 hours and process the complete erasure of your data from our internal databases and CRM within 30 days. You will receive a final confirmation code once deletion is complete.

Meta / Facebook App User Data Deletion

If you interacted with Sahajta AI through Meta Lead Ads or Facebook Login apps and wish to revoke application access and delete associated data:

  1. Go to your Facebook Account Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. Navigate to Apps and Websites.
  3. Find Sahajta AI / Enquiry-to-Site-Visit Engine in the list.
  4. Click Remove to revoke permissions and trigger automatic data deletion request callback.
