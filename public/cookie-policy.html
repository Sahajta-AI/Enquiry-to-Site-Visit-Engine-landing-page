Cookie Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: August 12, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Sahajta AI Solution Pvt Ltd (“Sahajta AI,” “We,” “Us,” or “Our”) uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website at www.sahajta.com or interact with our Enquiry-to-Site-Visit Engine tools and landing resources.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files downloaded to your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They allow websites to remember your actions, preferences, and browsing behavior over a period of time, making your interaction faster and more convenient.

2. How We Use Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and tracking tools for the following key purposes:

  • Essential & Functional Cookies: Necessary to run our website, enable navigation, manage form steps, and display scheduling widgets.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Measure visitor behavior, page views, and performance to help us optimize user experience.
  • Meta Pixel & Advertising Analytics: We utilize Meta Pixel (`fbq`) to measure campaign performance, track engagement with our intake modal, and optimize our advertising to real estate developers.

3. Third-Party Analytics Tools

We work with trusted third-party providers such as Google Analytics and Meta Ads. These providers may set cookies on your browser to collect generic, non-personally identifiable usage data. You can learn more about Google's practices by visiting Google Privacy Partners.

4. Managing & Disabling Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookie preferences through browser settings. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies or notify you when a cookie is sent. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact your ability to use certain features on our site, such as booking a call.
