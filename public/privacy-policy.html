2. What We Collect

There are two primary categories of information we obtain online:

Voluntarily Submitted Information:

When you participate in activities on our site—such as filling out our qualification form, scheduling a strategy call, or requesting information—you submit personal identifiers such as your full name, work email address, company or project name, and WhatsApp phone number. When you visit our resources, basic information is passively collected through your browser via cookies and analytics pixels (including Google Analytics and Meta Pixel). No mobile or SMS information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
